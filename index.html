Profile

Jessy Chia-Yu Lin (林家瑜) is a doctoral student in the Management of Information Systems at College of Commerce at National Chengchi University, Taiwan (國立政治大學資訊管理系). Her research interests focus on how advanced technologies impact individuals, organizations, and society. Her recent doctoral research involved an Action Design Research (ADR) project that applied Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) tools in rural areas to develop locally meaningful digital solutions.
