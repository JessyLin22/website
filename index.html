About Me 😊

Jessy Chia-Yu Lin (林家瑜) is a doctoral student in the Management Information Systems at College of Commerce at National Chengchi University (NCCU), Taiwan. Her research interests center on digital inclusion, digital for development, digital autonomy, AI implementation, and social media community across diverse social contexts. She has published in Information Systems Frontiers, Social Science and Medicine and serveral leading IS conferences.

Recent Works 🧐

Her recent work involves an action design research project that collaborates with rural communities to co-create digital solutions using emerging technologies, such as GenAI and no-code platforms, to advance community development. Prior to her doctoral studies, she investigated human behavior in online health communities by applying text mining techniques to multiple social media platforms.
