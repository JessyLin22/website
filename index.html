Jessy Chia-Yu Lin (林家瑜) is a doctoral student in the Management of Information Systems at College of Commerce at National Chengchi University (NCCU), Taiwan (國立政治大學資訊管理系). Her research interests center on the social changes and implications of IS/IT, including generative AI, online communities, and social media, with a particular interest in their impact on socially or digitally marginalized communities. She has published in leading information systems conferences such as International Conference of Information Systems (ICIS), European Group of Organizational Studies (EGOS), and UK academic for information systems (UKAIS).

Recent Works

Her recent works involve an action design research project that applied generative artificial intelligence tools in rural areas to develop locally meaningful digital solutions. Prior to her doctoral studies, she investigated human behavior in online health communities by applying text mining techniques to multiple social media platforms.
